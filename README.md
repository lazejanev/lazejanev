## About me

I am an independent **Dynamics 365 Solution Architect** and founder of **Janev Consulting**, based in Skopje, North Macedonia. Two decades in IT, in the **Dynamics ecosystem since 2008**, and still genuinely excited every time an ERP does something it could not do yesterday.

I am a **four-time Microsoft MVP** in the **AI ERP** area and a **Microsoft Certified Trainer**. I design and deliver large-scale D365 Finance and Operations implementations globally, with a strong focus on architecture, governance, and modern ALM.

- Specializing in **D365 Finance & Operations**, Supply Chain Management, Dual-Write, and Power Platform integration.
- Building the shift from traditional ERP to **AI ERP**: autonomous agents, Copilot, and MCP-driven X++ development.
- Automating everything I can in Azure DevOps: build, release, LCS asset upload, PPAC deployment, and tenant-wide app updates.
- Turning repetitive manual work into hands-off, repeatable pipelines so the humans focus on the problems that actually need a brain.
